A. Questions about writing a CV

In most cases a CV should fit on one page — especially if you have fewer than 8–10 years of experience. Two pages are acceptable for senior professionals and managers. Longer documents mainly apply to academic CVs.

In Europe, "CV" and "resume" mean the same thing. In the US, a resume is a short document tailored to a job offer, while a CV is a long academic document. If you're applying abroad — follow the language used in the job listing.

In Poland and Germany, a photo is common. In Spain — it's neutral. In the US and UK — it's discouraged. In LatweCV, the photo is an optional block you can turn on or off with a single click.
