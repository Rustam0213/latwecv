Build your CV from blocks, not a template

Drag a section onto the workspace, fill it with your data, and rearrange it however you like. Every block has its own editor — no more fighting with formatting in Word.

Multilingual

One CV, six language versions

Applying for a job abroad? Switch your CV to another language with a single click — section headers and labels translate automatically, while your data stays untouched.

  • Interface: Polish, English, German, Spanish, Ukrainian, Russian
  • The CV content language is independent of the interface language
  • Language proficiency levels on the CEFR scale (A1–C2 + native)
  • Section labels are translated automatically
